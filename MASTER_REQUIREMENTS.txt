# PART 31 — SAAS FINANCIAL FORMULAS

## MONTHLY RECURRING REVENUE

MRR = Sum of eligible active recurring subscriptions normalized to a monthly amount.

Exclude applicable taxes and one-time charges under the selected MRR policy.

## ANNUAL RECURRING REVENUE

ARR = MRR × 12.

## AVERAGE REVENUE PER USER

ARPU = Revenue for the period / Average eligible users for the same period, using a clearly defined policy.

## CUSTOMER CHURN

Customer Churn Rate = Eligible customers lost during the period / Eligible customers at the beginning of the period × 100.

## GROSS MARGIN

Gross Margin = (Revenue - Cost of Revenue) / Revenue × 100.

## NET PROFIT MARGIN

Net Profit Margin = Net Profit / Revenue × 100.

## CUSTOMER ACQUISITION COST

CAC = Eligible Sales and Marketing Acquisition Costs / New Customers Acquired, using a consistent period and attribution policy.

## CUSTOMER LIFETIME VALUE

Calculate customer lifetime value using a documented model that considers customer revenue, gross margin, retention, and customer lifespan.

Do not present uncertain estimates as verified facts.

## BREAK-EVEN CUSTOMER COUNT

Break-even Customers = Fixed Costs / Contribution per Paying Customer, when contribution is positive and assumptions are valid.
